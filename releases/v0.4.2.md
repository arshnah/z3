## What's new

### Artist & Album Pages
- **Detailed artist pages.** Browse an artist's popular songs (with **Show all songs** to expand their full catalog and a search filter), songs by them already in your library, **Albums**, **Singles & EPs**, **Playlists & features**, and **Fans might also like** (related artists).
- **Save albums & releases straight to your library.** Click any album, single, or artist playlist to open its full release page, or use the quick **Save to Library** button / context menu directly on any album card to save the entire release as a playlist with cover art or add all of its tracks to an existing playlist.
- **Click any artist anywhere — including multi-artist songs.** Everywhere an artist name appears (song rows, cards, Home hero and shelves, Search, Queue, Player capsule, Now Playing, and Stats), hovering highlights the specific artist under your cursor and clicking opens that artist's page. Combined credits like `"A, B, & C"` or `"A & B"` are split into individual clickable links too, and every song's `…` menu includes **Go to artist** and **Go to album**.
- **Artists & albums in Search.** Searching now shows an **Artists** strip at the top of the results and an **Albums & singles** shelf alongside songs on both YouTube Music and JioSaavn.

### Fixes & Improvements
- **Word-synced lyrics on Android:** Android now shares the full `lrclib` lookup with desktop, including `lyricsfile` word-by-word timings, title/artist search fallback, and upgrading tracks that only had unsynced embedded lyrics.
- **Android storage sharing:** Sharing a song on Android now uses the shared library storage instance rather than a separate instance.
- **Scrobbling on close:** Closing the app now flushes any in-progress listen that has already passed the scrobble threshold.
- **Paused lyric word fill:** Seeking or finishing a lyrics lookup while paused now updates the active line's word-by-word fill immediately.
