## What's new

- The Android home-screen widget can reopen Zeta and resume your last track after Android stops the app process. Its playback buttons also fit shorter widget layouts.
- Nearby pairing has a dedicated invitation field with keyboard submission. The QR scanner adds a framing guide and checks the camera feed at multiple scales.
- The equalizer has centered gain sliders with larger touch targets. The download queue, storage manager, and duplicate review now share one layout in Settings.
