## What's new since v0.4.3

### Desktop YouTube playback

- Zeta retries YouTube playback and downloads with [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/wiki/Installation) when the built-in request fails, including on HTTP 403. The fallback uses yt-dlp from your PATH and needs a supported [JavaScript runtime and YouTube challenge scripts](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/wiki/EJS).
- For videos that require sign-in, open **Settings → YouTube** and choose a signed-in browser profile or a Netscape-format `cookies.txt` file. Zeta remembers your choice and uses it when yt-dlp retries.

### Discovery and playback

- Find music on Home through personalized recommendations and genre collections. Search suggestions cover songs, artists, albums, playlists, and music videos.
- Radio and autoplay pick more varied tracks and avoid repeating known songs and artists.
- Choose streaming and download quality independently. Watch matching music videos from the player, with Zeta audio pausing while the video plays.
- Turn on **Lite mode** in Settings to reduce effects and animation on slower desktops.

### Android

- Browse downloaded music, playlists, favourites, and albums in Android Auto. Search and stream YouTube Music and JioSaavn while Zeta is open on the phone.
- Navigation, search results, and the player have been refined for narrow screens.
