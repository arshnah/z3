## What's new

### Playlist Imports & Library
- **Import M3U, Spotify, and YouTube playlists.** Import `.m3u` / `.m3u8` playlists (copying referenced local audio into the library when needed), Spotify playlists exported as CSV via Exportify (matched to YouTube Music tracks), and public YouTube playlist URLs — directly from the **Import playlist** button in **Library** or from **Settings → Music folder**.
- **Recursive local folder indexing.** Audio files in nested subfolders inside your music directory are now indexed automatically and surfaced in a `Music Folder` view.
- **Prefer local search results.** Optional toggle in **Settings → Playback** to play a matching downloaded song from your library when selecting a search result.
- **Smarter playlist recommendations.** Playlist recommendations now deduplicate against tracks already in the playlist across provider IDs, accents, punctuation, and featured artist variations.

### Discord Rich Presence & Scrobbling
- **Discord Rich Presence toggle & auto-reconnect.** Enable or disable Discord Rich Presence in **Settings → Playback**, with automatic reconnection when Discord restarts and Apple Music artwork lookup for local files without a YouTube video ID, while keeping the **`Listening to <Song>`** status header.
- **Improved scrobble metadata & Android FLAC imports.** Multi-artist metadata is cleaned and deduplicated for Last.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbles, and Android audio imports preserve `.flac` and other original extensions and MIME types.
