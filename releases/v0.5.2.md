## What's new

- **GitHub updates.** Zeta checks for new releases on desktop and Android. Supported desktop packages download updates in the background. On Android, install the verified APK through the system installer.
- **Desktop music videos.** The embedded YouTube player now identifies Zeta to YouTube, addressing error 153. Videos can also be opened on YouTube.
- **Library transfers.** Save a Zeta backup on desktop or Android and import it on the other device. Backups can carry playlists, favourites, listening history, and downloaded audio, up to 128 MiB.
- **Muzza on Android.** Import and export Muzza backups from Settings.
- **Android playback.** Fixed playback of downloaded tracks and prevented stale Android player updates from reversing play or pause taps.
- **Nearby devices.** Pair over the same Wi-Fi using QR or pairing codes. Send a Zeta backup directly to another device, control playback remotely, or invite others to a listening party.
- **Backup preview.** Review what a backup contains before importing. After import, queue missing audio and review possible duplicates.
- **Library and listening.** Manage offline storage and the download queue in Settings. Search listening history, revisit forgotten favourites, and share a monthly listening poster.
- **Player controls.** Preload the next two tracks, adjust the equalizer and volume leveling, and use the Zeta home-screen widget on Android.
